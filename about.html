OUR MISSION

WHY WE EXIST

Wildlife Encyclopedia is an educational project created to help people discover the diversity of animals around the world. Our goal is to make information about wildlife simple, interesting and easy to explore.

We believe that learning about animals can help people understand ecosystems and become more aware of the importance of protecting nature.
